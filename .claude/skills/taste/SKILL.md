Apply [the shared Start workflow](../../../prompts/START_MODERNIZATION.md) and
[technical contract](../../../docs/TECHNICAL_REFERENCE.md) first. This is an
adapted instruction-only skill, not the full upstream plugin. It grants no tools
or permissions. Keep raw source/PII protected; use synthetic or explicitly
approved sanitized views. Preserve legacy behavior, immutable evidence, one
Coordinator writer and actual human approval. Do not install or invoke upstream
runtimes, MCP servers, hooks, proxies or background services.

The pinned upstream taste-skill targets landing pages and portfolios and explicitly excludes dashboards and multi-step product UI. This local adaptation uses only its audience/brief and restrained-design principles; it does not apply marketing layouts or motion presets to Workbench.

Identify the surface as a regulated, task-oriented analyst tool or an executive evidence report. Preserve the existing Wells Fargo-oriented palette, usable controls and factual content. Professional clarity, accessibility and consistent components outrank visual novelty.

Use typography, spacing and hierarchy to show the primary action and the evidence relationship. Avoid decorative gradients, gratuitous animation, competing accents and generic promotional cards that bury the actual result.

Refinement must retain the operator's current state, selected requirements and discoverable next step. Verify long content, filtered results, code excerpts and mobile/zoom reflow with synthetic data. Do not turn an excluded or unverified result into celebratory success styling.

Adapted scope: audience-aware visual review only. New icon/font/image dependencies, broad marketing redesign, styling presets, runtimes and upstream scripts are excluded. No official enterprise brand approval is implied.
